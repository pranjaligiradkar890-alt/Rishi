The Rose & The Boy

A story about two people who did not realise, at first, that they were becoming each other's favourite chapter.

PROLOGUE

Before I knew
what you would become.

Every love story has a beginning. Ours simply didn't know it was one.

There are moments in life that seem completely insignificant while they are happening, only to become sacred when remembered later.

Perhaps that is the cruelest and most beautiful thing about time: it never tells us which moments deserve to be held onto.

If it had told me, back then, that you would eventually become one of the most tender and irreplaceable parts of my life, I might have looked at you differently.

I might have paid closer attention. I might have memorised the beginning. But perhaps it is better that I didn't. Because love, when it is real, does not always arrive like thunder. Sometimes it arrives quietly. It takes a seat beside you. It becomes a conversation. Then a habit. Then a comfort. Then a person whose absence feels strangely louder than the presence of everyone else. And one day, without noticing when it happened, you realise: you have become home to someone.

CHAPTER I

The boy who
became my world.

Rishi, I don't think you understand the magnitude of what you have become to me.

You are not merely someone I love. You have become part of the way I experience my days. There are things I want to tell you first. Things that happen and somehow my mind immediately thinks, “I need to tell him this.” There are jokes that aren't funny until I imagine telling you. There are moments that feel incomplete because I cannot share them with you. And there are nights when the world becomes quiet enough for me to realise just how deeply you have settled into my heart.

You became important to me gradually. And perhaps that is why it became so profound. You weren't a sudden storm. You were rain. Quiet. Persistent. Eventually everywhere.

“I did not know I was looking for you; I only knew that something felt different after you arrived.”

— FROM THE UNFINISHED MANUSCRIPT OF US

CHAPTER III

If you ever wondered
how deeply I love you.

Then read this slowly.

I love you in a way that is difficult to compress into a sentence.

Not because the feeling is complicated, but because it is enormous.

It exists in places language sometimes cannot reach.

I love you in the quiet moments, when nothing extraordinary is happening and yet the thought of having you in my life feels like something extraordinary in itself.

I love you in the middle of ordinary days. In the middle of laughter. In the middle of irritation. In the middle of conversations that make no sense. I love you when you are at your best, and I love the imperfect, unpolished parts of you that perhaps you don't think deserve love quite as much.

I don't love an imaginary, flawless version of you. I love you as a person. Entirely. Humanly. Tenderly. With all the beautiful contradictions that make you, you.

And if you ever wonder what it means to be loved by me, I hope you understand this:

It means that somewhere inside my heart, there is a place where your name has stopped feeling like a name and started feeling like home.

I don't promise that life will always be perfect. I don't promise that we will never misunderstand each other. But I can promise you that what I feel for you is not something I want to treat casually.

Because you matter to me. More than these pages can explain. More than a photograph can preserve. More than one anniversary can possibly contain.

And if I had a thousand different ways to tell you that I love you, I would probably use all of them. Then invent another thousand. Just to make sure you understood.

— the girl who loves you ♡
CHAPTER IV

The little things
you will never know I notice.

I notice more than I tell you.

I notice the small changes in your voice. The little habits you probably assume nobody remembers. The moments when you are pretending everything is fine. The moments when you are genuinely happy. The ridiculous things you do without realising how much they make me smile.

I notice because I love you. And loving someone, I think, is partly the art of paying attention.

The world may know your name. But I want to know your little things.

The things that don't make it into photographs. The things that don't belong on social media. The things that exist only in the private language two people create when they become important to one another.

CHAPTER V

Questions I would only
ask my boyfriend.

No overthinking. No diplomatic answers. Just you.

02

03

04

When you imagine your happiest future, do you secretly see me in it?

05

If we were old, annoying and still arguing over stupid things, would you still hold my hand?

06

Do you believe these six months are only the beginning of our story?

CHAPTER VI

A few adventures
for the next chapters.

One entire day with no carefully constructed itinerary. Just us, somewhere, discovering what happens.

Write me one thing you've never managed to say properly. Something honest enough to make you nervous.

Choose one of our old photographs and recreate it years from now. Let's see how much changes—and how much doesn't.

Tell me about one completely ordinary Tuesday you want us to have together someday.

Come back to this website years from now and read it again. Then tell me whether I was right about us.

CHAPTER VII

Things I never
say out loud.

Open the first letter
Sometimes I look at you and quietly wonder how someone can become so important without even realising it.
Open the second letter
I hope you never become so familiar with my love that you stop realising how extraordinary it is to be loved this deeply.
Open the final letter
If I could give you one thing, it would be the ability to see yourself through my eyes for just five minutes. Then you would finally understand why I stayed.
CHAPTER VIII

The chapters
we haven't written yet.

There are so many versions of us that haven't happened yet.

The us that has travelled somewhere neither of us has seen. The us that has laughed about a memory we haven't created. The us that has survived difficult seasons and celebrated beautiful ones. The us that knows each other even better than we do now.

I don't know exactly what our future will look like.

And strangely, I don't need to. Because sometimes the most beautiful part of loving someone is not knowing every detail of tomorrow— only knowing who you hope will be standing beside you when tomorrow arrives.

And when I imagine mine, I still find you there.

EPILOGUE

Don't call this
the end. It's only the first volume.

Six months ago, we were simply two people living our separate ordinary lives. Now there is an entire little universe that exists between us. A universe made of memories. Of conversations. Of photographs. Of mistakes. Of laughter. Of tenderness. Of you. Of me. Of us.

And if one day you ever question whether you were loved— come back here. Read these pages again. Look at the photographs. Remember the little things. Remember that somewhere in this enormous world, there was a girl who looked at you and thought:

“I could spend a lifetime writing about him, and still run out of pages.”
