01 — our story

It wasn't always easy.

Six months sounds like such a small amount of time, but when I think about everything we've lived through, it feels like we've collected an entire little universe together.

The silly conversations. The arguments. The random laughs. The nights when everything felt too much. The moments when we didn't know what would happen next.

Somehow, through all of it, there was still us.

♥ ✿ ♥ ✿ ♥
02 — a letter for you

I'm proud of you.

I don't think I tell you enough how proud I am of you.

I've seen the version of you that everyone else sees, but I've also seen the tired version. The confused version. The version that doesn't know what to do next. The version that keeps going anyway.

And honestly? That version of you deserves love too.

I don't want to only stand beside you when everything is beautiful and easy. I want to be the person who reminds you that you're still worthy when life gets messy.

You don't have to have everything figured out for me to believe in you.

always rooting for you, ♡
03 — my promise

I'll be beside you.

I can't promise that every day will be perfect.

I can't promise that we'll never disagree, or that life will always be kind to us.

But I can promise that when things become difficult, I'll try to remember the person I fell in love with underneath all the temporary chaos.

I'll celebrate your little victories. I'll listen to your bad days. I'll laugh at your stupid jokes. I'll annoy you for absolutely no reason.

And I'll keep making little spaces like this just to remind you:

you are loved here.
