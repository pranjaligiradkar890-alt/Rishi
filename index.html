Dear you...

There are things I don't say enough.

I don't know how to perfectly explain everything I feel for you. Sometimes I have so many things inside my head that even the right words don't come out.

But if there is one thing I want you to understand, it's that you became important to me in a way I never expected.

At first, I didn't know what to believe. I didn't know whether I should trust everything. I had doubts, fears and so many questions.

I didn't have blind trust... I had fear.

And honestly? I'm still learning. I'm trying to understand. I'm trying to trust. I'm trying to let my heart believe in something instead of constantly preparing itself for disappointment.

Because sometimes trusting someone isn't a switch that suddenly turns on. Sometimes it's a hundred tiny moments. One honest conversation. One promise kept. One difficult day survived together. One moment where someone chooses to stay.

And I'm trying. I'm genuinely trying.

Not because I don't have fears. But because you matter enough to me that I don't want my fears to decide everything for my heart.

— yours, with all my complicated feelings ♡

But underneath all of that is someone who genuinely cares about you.

Someone who wants to see you happy. Someone who wants to see you succeed. Someone who wants to be proud of you. Someone who wants to look back one day and say...

"We made it through all those difficult moments."
